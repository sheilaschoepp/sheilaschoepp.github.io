RLJ is a peer-reviewed journal dedicated to reinforcement learning, publishing a new volume each quarter from submissions reviewed on a rolling basis. Review weighs methodological rigor over perceived importance, and accepted papers can be presented at the associated Reinforcement Learning Conference (RLC).
